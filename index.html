I am Jiake Ge, an Assistant Researcher at the College of Intelligence and Computing, Tianjin University. I received my PhD in 2024 from Renmin University of China, under the supervision of Professor Yunpeng Chai. I currently serve as an Executive Committee Member for the CCF Database Committee and Information Systems Committee. I have long been dedicated to research in intelligent database technology (AI4DB) and intelligent data management technology. I have published over 10 papers in important domestic and international academic conferences and journals such as SIGMOD, ICDE, and WWW, and I serve as a reviewer for journals and conferences like TKDE. I have been selected for the 2025 China Computer Federation Information Systems Committee PhD Thesis Incentive Program, the National Postdoctoral Researcher Program, the Joint Funding from China Postdoctoral Science Foundation and Tianjin City (Special Funding), and the Tianjin City Postdoctoral Innovation Position Funding. Additionally, I received the Best Paper Award at the 3rd International Workshop on Large-Scale Graph Data Analytics (LSGDA 2024) and funding from Renmin University of China's Outstanding Innovative Talent Program. I host one provincial/ministerial scientific research project and one teaching reform project.
Recruiting Master's students for 2026, welcome students interested in my research directions to contact me!
