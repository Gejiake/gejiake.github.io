Jiake Ge is an Assistant Researcher at the College of Intelligence and Computing, Tianjin University, Executive Committee Member of CCF Database Committee, Executive Committee Member of CCF Information System Committee. He has long been engaged in research on intelligent database technology (AI4DB) and database systems. He has published more than 10 papers in important domestic and international academic conferences and journals such as SIGMOD, ICDE, WWW, and served as a reviewer for journals and conferences such as TKDE. He has been selected for the 2025 China Computer Federation Information System Committee Doctoral Dissertation Incentive Program, 2025 National Postdoctoral Researcher Funding Program, the 3rd Batch of China Postdoctoral Science Foundation and Tianjin Joint Funding (Special Funding), Tianjin Postdoctoral Innovation Position Funding, Best Paper Award at the 3rd International Workshop on Large-Scale Graph Data Analytics (LSGDA 2024), Renmin University of China Outstanding Funded Innovative Talent Program. He hosts one provincial-level project and one teaching reform project.
